## Who I am

I am Yen-Chung Chen, and this is my personal website ([https://www.yenchungchen.com/](https://www.yenchungchen.com/)). For this website, I can be contacted at [ycc520@nyu.edu](mailto:ycc520@nyu.edu).

I am not an expert in website technology or privacy law, so this page describes how the site is built and what I understand it to do. It is meant as a practical privacy notice, not legal advice.

The website is written with [Hugo](https://gohugo.io/). The source is hosted on [GitHub](https://github.com/chenyenchung/personal_site), the static site is built and hosted by [Netlify](https://www.netlify.com/), and public traffic is served through [Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com/) as a CDN and security layer.

Last updated: 2026-04-26.

## What this site does not do

This site does not use Google Analytics, advertising pixels, behavioral advertising tools, comment systems, accounts, newsletter signup forms, or contact forms. I do not intentionally identify visitors or build profiles of individual readers.

The site itself does not set analytics cookies, and the local JavaScript used for navigation and search does not use `localStorage` or `sessionStorage`.

## Hosting, CDN, and server logs

Like most websites, this site cannot be delivered without processing ordinary web request information. Because the site is hosted on Netlify and served through Cloudflare, those providers may process technical information such as your IP address, requested URL, browser user agent, referring page, timestamps, cache and routing data, security events, and similar request metadata.

I use these services so the site can be delivered reliably, cached close to readers, redirected from older domains, protected from abuse, and debugged when something breaks. If privacy laws such as the GDPR or UK GDPR apply, my understanding is that this processing is based on the legitimate interest of operating and securing a public website.

Netlify and Cloudflare may process this data outside your country. Their current privacy and data processing materials are available here:

- [Netlify Privacy Statement](https://www.netlify.com/privacy/) and [Netlify Data Processing Agreement](https://www.netlify.com/pdf/netlify-dpa.pdf)
- [Cloudflare Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/), [Cloudflare Data Processing Addendum](https://www.cloudflare.com/cloudflare-customer-dpa/), and [Cloudflare GDPR information](https://www.cloudflare.com/trust-hub/gdpr/)

I do not use server or CDN logs to identify individual visitors. I may see aggregate operational information or security events made available by these providers, but I do not combine them with other data to identify you. Retention of hosting and CDN logs is controlled mainly by the providers and by the service configuration.

## Cookies

The site itself does not set tracking cookies. Cloudflare may set strictly necessary cookies when needed for security, bot mitigation, rate limiting, challenge pages, load balancing, or similar CDN functions. These cookies are used to deliver and protect the site, not for my advertising or analytics. Cloudflare describes these cookies in its [Cloudflare Cookies documentation](https://developers.cloudflare.com/fundamentals/reference/policies-compliances/cloudflare-cookies/).

Most browsers let you block cookies. The regular pages of this site should remain readable, although blocking necessary security cookies could affect access if Cloudflare asks your browser to complete a security check.

## Search

Search on this site runs in your browser. The search page downloads a static `/index.json` file generated from public site content and filters it locally.

Search queries are placed in the page URL as the `q` parameter so search links can be shared or reloaded. That means a search query may appear in browser history and in ordinary request logs handled by Netlify or Cloudflare when the search URL is requested.

## Fonts, slides, and external links

The main site currently loads fonts from [Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com/). When your browser requests those font files, Google receives request information such as your IP address, the requested font URL, user agent, and referrer. Google says the Google Fonts API does not set or log cookies and does not use the information to create end-user profiles or for targeted advertising; see Google's [Google Fonts privacy FAQ](https://developers.google.com/fonts/faq/privacy).

Some pages link to external sites such as Google Scholar, GitHub, Bluesky, Twitter/X, Mastodon, ORCID, DOI publishers, journals, and other resources. If you follow those links, the destination site receives your request and handles it under its own privacy policy.

One linked standalone slide deck under `/slides/` was generated separately from the main Hugo theme. When opened, it may load third-party resources such as Google Fonts, MathJax from `mathjax.rstudio.com`, and remote images. Those third parties may receive ordinary request metadata from your browser.

## If you contact me

If you email me, I will receive your email address, the contents of your message, and any other information you choose to include. I use that information to read and respond to your message. I may keep email correspondence as needed for ordinary record keeping, follow-up, or legal reasons.

## Your choices and rights

You can read the site without creating an account or sending me personal information. You can also use browser tools or extensions to block third-party resources, cookies, or referrer headers, although that may change how the site looks or works.

If privacy laws that grant individual rights apply to you, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or portability of personal data, and you may have a right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Because this is a static personal site, I may not have visitor data that can reasonably be linked back to you, but you can contact me at [ycc520@nyu.edu](mailto:ycc520@nyu.edu) with privacy questions or requests.

If I materially change how this site handles visitor data, I will update this notice.

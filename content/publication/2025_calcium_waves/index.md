abstract = "The research demonstrates that spontaneous calcium waves arise among non-neuronal support cells in the developing Drosophila eye to drive retinal morphogenesis. The waves are initiated through Cad96Ca receptor signaling and propagate via gap junctions among retinal cells while excluding photoreceptors. The mechanism enables size-dependent myosin II-driven contractions that compensate for boundary irregularities, ensuring uniform ommatidial packing that is critical for precise optical architecture."

This project focuses on how spatial domains, temporal transcription factors, and tissue-scale developmental programs generate the diversity and proportions of neurons in the Drosophila visual system.

The work connects developmental atlases, gene regulatory mechanisms, cell-type-specific tools, and studies of how the eye and optic lobe achieve reproducible neural patterning.
